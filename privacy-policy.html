Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Reverential AV Integration Private Limited ("Reverential", "we", "us", "our") operates the website at reverentialav.in. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide: When you submit a consultation request or request the 12-point sound system check, we collect your name, organisation, email address, phone number, and any message you include. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and to contact you about your project.

Analytics data: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors find and use this site. GA4 collects anonymised information including pages visited, time on site, device type, and approximate geographic location. This data does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics by accepting analytics tracking below or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Email automation: When you request the 12-point sound system check, your email address is processed by EmailJS to send you the checklist link. EmailJS does not retain your email address beyond the delivery of that email.

2. How we use your information

We use the information you submit to: respond to your consultation enquiry; send you the 12-point sound system check you requested; follow up on project discussions you initiate; and improve our service based on aggregated analytics data. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share your enquiry details with the following service providers solely to operate this website:

Web3Forms — processes form submissions and forwards them to our inbox. Web3Forms privacy policy →

EmailJS — delivers the checklist email. EmailJS privacy policy →

Google Analytics — provides anonymised website analytics. Google privacy policy →

Cloudflare — hosts this website and provides security services. Cloudflare privacy policy →

4. Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information. You can control cookie use through your browser settings or by declining analytics cookies on your first visit.

5. Data retention

Enquiry emails are retained in our inbox for as long as is necessary to respond to and complete your project. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics for 14 months by default. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us.

6. Your rights

You have the right to: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of your data; withdraw consent for analytics at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@reverentialav.in.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
