Two sounds reach every listener.

Nearly every speech problem in a room comes down to the balance between them.

Direct sound

Travels straight from the loudspeaker to the ear. It carries the words, and it weakens the further back you sit.

Reflected sound

The same sound returning off walls, ceiling and floor as hundreds of overlapping copies, arriving slightly late. It stays strong everywhere in the room.

At the back, the direct sound has faded but the reflections have not, so one word smears into the next. Turning up the volume raises both together. The fix is in the room and in the system design.

The problems are predictable.

They appear in nearly every room the moment a system is expected to work.

  • • The system only works when it is turned up enough that feedback becomes a problem
  • • Speech is clear at the front of the room but becomes indistinct at the back, and the operator is blamed
  • • The room rings at a particular frequency no amount of equipment will fix
  • • The system only works when the one person trained on it is there

Spaces we work in.

How the work is sequenced.

Each phase builds on the one before. The outcome is documentation and a system that a capable person can run without mystery.

You receive

A written site survey delivered by the engineer who took the measurements, with photographs, a room schematic and a clear description of what is acoustically or operationally wrong.

You receive

A model of the space showing reverberation time, predicted system response and the effect of proposed acoustic treatments.

You receive

A block diagram showing every component, its connection, the calculated signal flow, and the loading on every amplifier and processor.

You receive

Rigging to load-rated standards with written load calculations. All cable runs documented and labelled. All amplifier locations and heat-load analysis calculated.

You receive

Real-time measurements from the positions your audience occupies. A comparison of actual versus predicted performance. Any gap smaller than the prediction is documented and signed off.

You receive

A complete system manual showing signal flow, gain structure, every processing step, and the reasoning behind it. Written hand-over training with the team that will operate the system daily.
